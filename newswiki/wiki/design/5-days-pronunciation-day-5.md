# [5 days to improve your pronunciation / Day 5 - You've earned your certificate](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/themed_weeks_pronunciation/ep-201127)

## Core View
- This is the final day of Tim's 5-day pronunciation challenge, with no homework assigned.
- Users are encouraged to watch Episode 4 of the Inspector Stone mystery to find out who stole the ring.
- Episode 4 is linked directly: [[episodes/inspector-stone-episode-4|Episode 4 - The Ring Thief Is...]]
- Tim recommends practicing pronunciation by recording oneself and reviewing the audio, which supports self-directed learning and feedback loops.
- Key resources include the BBC Learning English Pronunciation Workshop and The Sounds of English, both of which are linked for further practice: [[hubs/bbc-learning-english|BBC Learning English]] and [[resources/pronunciation-workshop|Tim's Pronunciation Workshop]].

## Key Takeaways
- Practise pronunciation by recording and reviewing your speech.
- Use physical or online dictionaries to learn new word pronunciations.
- Improve clarity by speaking slowly and clearly, not necessarily achieving native-level fluency.
- Engage with interactive content such as the Inspector Stone mystery to build listening and comprehension skills.
