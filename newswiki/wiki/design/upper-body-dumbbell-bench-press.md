# [Upper Body Dumbbell Bench Press: Step-by-Step Guide for Proper Form](https://www.calorietech.com/exercises/5763d3b011fc5077c3acf58b)

## Core View
- The exercise is performed on an inclined bench at 30 degrees to reduce strain on the lower back and improve upper body engagement.
- Key movement cues include keeping the shoulders retracted, the upper back pressed against the bench, and the elbows aligned vertically to maintain stability.
- The exercise emphasizes controlled movement: a 1-second pause at the top, slow descent to the chest level, and consistent breathing patterns (inhale during descent, exhale during ascent).
- Common mistakes include shoulder forward movement and improper elbow positioning, which can lead to shoulder impingement or poor form.

## Movement Instructions
- Begin by adjusting the bench to 30 degrees, feet firmly planted on the ground, and core engaged.
- Hands grip dumbbells with palms facing forward, arms vertical, and elbows aligned with the body.
- On the upward phase, push the dumbbells toward the midline of the body, elbows slightly bent at the peak to maintain control.
- On the downward phase, lower the dumbbells slowly to the level of the chest, keeping the arms vertical and the back pressed against the bench throughout.
