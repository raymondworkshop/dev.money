# [Principles For Living: An Operating System for Life](https://fs.blog/principles/)

## 1. Direction Over Speed
- Prioritize correct direction over rapid movement; progress is positive regardless of speed if the destination is clear.
- Avoid abandoning a chosen path due to temporary stops; trust the process and long-term progress.
- Focus on charting a course and staying the course, rather than constantly seeking the illusion of progress.
- [AI Synthesis] Harrington Emerson quote emphasizes understanding principles over merely applying methods.

## 2. Live Deliberately
- Avoid settling into habits and repeating years without intentional living; life happens now.
- Cultivate a life and mind of purpose, independent of external influences, to avoid regret at the end of life.
- Embrace awareness and purposeful action to craft each day deliberately for a life brimming with joy and fulfillment.
- Take immediate action on dreams, learning, relationships, and shedding burdens.

## 3. Thoughtful Opinions Held Loosely
- Be willing to update beliefs when faced with contradictory facts, rather than rationalizing or refuting new information.
- Strive for a balance between conviction in actions and humility to admit being wrong.
- Recognize that no one has a perfect understanding; adapt and change as understanding grows to achieve desired life outcomes.
- Avoid filtering out conflicting evidence, which leads to ignorance and sacrificing correctness for being 'right'.

## 4. Principles Outlive Tactics
- Understand the underlying principles behind tactics to adapt and course-correct effectively, similar to a coach in American football.
- Play-stealers who only adopt tactics without understanding principles lack the ability to adjust when plays fail.
- [AI Synthesis] Steve Jobs quote highlights the importance of following one's own heart and intuition over dogma and others' opinions.

## 5. Own Your Actions
- Accepting mistakes and seeking correction is crucial for significant life advancement.
- Refusing to own mistakes warps the feedback loop necessary for future decision-making.
- If one is the common denominator in recurring problems, failure to acknowledge this prevents change.
- [AI Synthesis] Stephen Covey's concept of proactive people not blaming circumstances allows for control over one's life direction.

## Key Takeaways
- Prioritize clear direction over speed.
- Live intentionally and purposefully each day.
- Maintain intellectual humility and be open to updating beliefs.
- Understand underlying principles, not just tactics, for lasting success.
- Take responsibility for your actions and mistakes to drive personal growth.
