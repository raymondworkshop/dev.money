# [他们说随身带着这些东西可缓解焦虑](https://cn.wsj.com/articles/panic-pouch-ae69b5aa?mod=cn_hp_wknd_pos1)

## 核心观点
- 伊莎贝拉·巴诺斯（22岁）使用Speks磁力小球解压玩具、酸味糖果、薰衣草精油、便携风扇和Aegis Formulas抗焦虑口含片来应对焦虑。
- 艾玛·斯特拉赫曼（39岁）携带除臭湿巾、劳拉西泮（Ativan）、Arrae缓解胀气胶囊、1毫克THC软糖、创可贴、卫生棉条、Diptyque香水小样和Pique抹茶粉等物品。
- 杰伊·埃米（10岁）使用便便造型减压球、迷你记事本、磁性石头、橡皮糖质感蚯蚓玩具和指尖陀螺作为儿童情绪调节工具。
- 埃莱妮·索洛穆·沃尔夫（47岁）随身携带指缘修护霜、小发刷（用于穴位按摩）和南非醉茄减压丸以缓解焦虑。
- [AI Synthesis] The trend of carrying a 'panic pouch' reflects a growing cultural emphasis on accessible, tangible self-care tools for managing anxiety, indicating a shift toward personal, non-medicalized mental health practices.

## 核心要点
- Anxiety is increasingly being managed through physical, everyday items rather than clinical interventions.
- Items like magnetic balls, essential oils, and calming scents are being used as accessible tools for emotional regulation.
- The practice of carrying a 'panic pouch' has become a form of peer support and identity expression among individuals with anxiety.
- [AI Synthesis] This behavior suggests a broader societal trend toward self-directed mental wellness, where personal items serve as both emotional anchors and social signals of shared experience.
