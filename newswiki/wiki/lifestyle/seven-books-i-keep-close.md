# [Seven books I keep close because I love them](https://blog.plover.com/2026/08/02/)

## Core View
- The author maintains a curated 'elbow shelf' of seven books intended to infuse his writing with specific intellectual emanations and provide conceptual inspiration.
- Roget's Thesaurus (4th ed) is utilized not as a synonym list, but as a hierarchical classification of the world to help refine the precision of ideas.
- The collection emphasizes the transition from the 'cramped' thinking of the Middle Ages to the 'joyfully brave' inquiry of the early Renaissance, exemplified by the works of Sir Thomas Browne and Boccaccio.
- The selection includes foundational texts in mathematical logic (Frege to Gödel) and early educational visual design (Comenius's *Orbis Pictus*), reflecting a multidisciplinary approach to knowledge.
- The Bible (NIV) is kept as a necessary cultural cornerstone for understanding the social and historical fabric of 21st-century America.
- [AI Synthesis] The 'elbow shelf' serves as a physical manifestation of a cognitive map, where the proximity of diverse texts (from medieval art to formal logic) facilitates cross-pollination of ideas during the creative process.

- Intellectual curation can act as a psychological anchor, providing a 'universe of discourse' that supports a writer's specific mental state.
- Visual and structured learning, as seen in [[design/_index|Design]] precursors like Comenius, remains a powerful method for direct and pithy communication.
