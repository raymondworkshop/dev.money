# [5 days to improve your pronunciation / Day 5 - You've earned your certificate](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/themed_weeks_pronunciation/ep-201127)

## Core View
- Prioritize clarity and intelligibility over achieving perfect native-speaker pronunciation to ensure effective communication in [[career/_index|Career]] contexts.
- Utilize a multi-modal approach to learning: use phonetic symbols, consult physical or online dictionaries, and practice in front of a mirror for visual feedback.
- Leverage modern technology by recording speech via smartphones or webcams to listen back and identify areas for improvement.
- Maintain detailed notes on pronunciation when acquiring new vocabulary and utilize resources from [[BBC Learning English]] for continued practice.

## Key Takeaways
- Aim for clarity and a steady pace rather than native-level perfection.
- Use recording tools and mirrors to monitor and refine speech patterns.
- Consistent use of phonetic symbols and dictionaries is key to mastering new word pronunciations.
