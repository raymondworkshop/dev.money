# [Facing AI-Driven Job Disruption: A New Initiative Aims to Prepare Millions of Americans](https://cn.wsj.com/articles/the-new-push-to-ready-millions-for-ai-career-upheaval-ee1273ff?mod=cn_hp_life_pos1)

## Core View
- A bipartisan coalition called RAISE US has been formed to address the growing threat of AI-induced job displacement.
- The initiative includes major employers like Amazon, Microsoft, Bank of America, and Eli Lilly, as well as state governments and nonprofit organizations.
- Led by former U.S. Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo and former Indiana Governor Eric Holcomb, RAISE US aims to expand retraining programs and reform long-standing policies like unemployment benefits.
- The group will test new labor transition models, including incentivizing employers to retain workers affected by AI automation.
- It will explore policy adjustments—such as modifying unemployment benefits—to support workers who leave jobs due to AI disruption and pursue AI-driven entrepreneurship.

## Key Focus Areas
- The initiative emphasizes retraining for white-collar professionals, who are particularly vulnerable to AI automation compared to manual laborers.
- State-level implementations vary: Maryland will expand a service-year program targeting healthcare and other growing fields; Arkansas will pilot an AI-driven career navigation platform.
- The group highlights that AI-driven labor shifts are more profound than past disruptions like globalization or offshoring.

## AI and Labor Policy Implications
- The article notes that while significant investment has gone into AI technology (e.g., chips, models), there remains insufficient focus on protecting labor futures.
- Leaders such as Brad Smith of Microsoft stress that no single entity can solve this issue—systemic, cross-institutional collaboration is essential.
- [AI] is not seen as a threat to human work, but as a force that requires proactive adaptation and policy innovation.

## Key Takeaways
- AI is disrupting white-collar jobs at an unprecedented scale, requiring coordinated labor and policy responses.
- RAISE US aims to unify public and private stakeholders to develop scalable, evidence-based transition strategies.
- Policy reforms—such as adjusting unemployment benefits for AI-affected workers—are being explored to support entrepreneurship and job mobility.
