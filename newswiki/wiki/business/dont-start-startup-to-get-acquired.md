# [Don't start a startup with the goal of getting acquired.](https://www.raymondyxu.com/blog/dontStartAStartupToGetAcquired)

## Core View
- Starting a company with the primary goal of being acquired (acqui-hire) is logically flawed due to the extremely low probability of success.
- The estimated overall probability of being acquired is roughly 0.1% (calculated as a 1% chance of entering [[hubs/yc|Y Combinator]] multiplied by a ~10% acquisition rate thereafter).
- For a target payout of $5M, a high-paying Big Tech job is a significantly more reliable path than the founder route, as the probability of reaching that financial milestone via employment is much higher than 0.1%.
- Even for those already in YC, the probability of securing a high-TC corporate role (>50%) outweighs the 10% chance of a $5M acqui-hire.
- [AI Synthesis] The risk-reward asymmetry of startups only justifies the venture if the goal is massive scale ('go big or go home'), rather than a modest exit that could be achieved with lower risk in a corporate career.

## Key Takeaways
- Acqui-hiring is a statistically poor strategy for wealth accumulation compared to high-TC employment in [[career/_index|Career]] paths.
- Founders should avoid short-term 'cash out' mindsets and instead focus on high-growth potential.
