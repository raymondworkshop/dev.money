# [Doing Something That’s Never Been Done Before](https://talglobus.com/p/doing-something-thats-never-been-done-before/)

## Core View
- People have limited time and energy, so projects that take long or are complex are less likely to be completed by others.
- Difficulty and step count reduce the likelihood of replication: the more steps required, the fewer people can complete them end-to-end.
- [AI Synthesis] Obscurity increases the chance of being first: rare or niche ideas are less likely to be discovered by others.
- [AI Synthesis] Progressive dependency creates exponential rarity: each step requires completion of the prior one, reducing the probability of sequential discovery.
- To maximize first-mover odds, prioritize obscurity, time consumption, difficulty, and unknowns that only resolve upon progress.

## Key Takeaways
- To increase the likelihood of being first, focus on obscurity, time investment, difficulty, and progressive dependencies.
- First-mover advantage is not just about innovation speed but about structural barriers to replication.
- The human factor—time, attention, and cognitive load—is a key constraint in innovation diffusion.
